Vickrey Field

Seal what you saw

Identify with a swappable open-weight model, seal the observation with your device key, and chain it to the one before. Sync later to anchor a commitment, signature, timestamp, coarse location, and species summary; the raw photo and notes are not anchored. A hosted model receives the photo for inference.

  1. Open-weight ID, local or hosted
  2. Signed and chained on the device
  3. Anchored and verified together
Vickrey seals the observation at capture; the ledger keeps the commitment. Editing the log cracks the seal — the ledger never changes, the copy does. Apache-2.0.
