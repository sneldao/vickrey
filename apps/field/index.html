Seal what you saw

Ledger: mock Hornet demo

A sighting written down today can be quietly changed tomorrow, and nobody can tell. Here, a model suggests what you saw, you confirm or correct it, and the record is signed on this device. Any later edit cracks the seal.

The model only suggests; the observer decides, and that decision is sealed with the record. A seal proves the record was not edited since — not that the identification is right. Apache-2.0.
