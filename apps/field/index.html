A sealed bid on what you saw

Ledger: mock Hornet demo

A field note written down today can be quietly changed tomorrow, and nobody can tell. Here, an open-weight model suggests what you saw, you confirm or correct it, and your decision is signed on this device and chained to the last entry. Any later edit cracks the seal.

The model only suggests; the observer decides, and that decision is sealed with the record. Verification detects changes against that seal; it does not establish the capture time or identification's accuracy. Apache-2.0.
