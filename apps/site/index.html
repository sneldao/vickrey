THE MECHANISM & THE WEDGE

    
One primitive across edge industries. Starting with the largest open commons on Earth.

    

      The name borrows the idea of a sealed bid. Here, a signature and a cryptographic commitment make changes detectable against the sealed record; they do not prove that an observation is true.
    

  


  

    

      01 · IDENTIFY
      
Open-weight suggestion

      
An open-weight model can suggest an identification through a reachable server. The observer confirms or corrects it. Offline, on-phone model execution is planned.

    

    

      02 · PROVE
      
Ed25519 seal & hash chain

      
Your confirmation or correction is signed on the device and hash-chained to the previous entry. Rewriting a queued record breaks the chain, and sync refuses to send it.

    

    

      03 · SHIELD
      
Anchor commitments, not raw data

      
The default anchor carries a commitment in an encrypted Zcash memo. Photos and coordinates stay off the public chain. A recipient can check a shared record's signature locally and ask the service to check its memo; this does not prove capture time.

    

    

      04 · CONTEXT
      
Public context stays outside the seal

      
Optional web lookups show nearby reports beside an anchored record, never inside it. Third-party search results can never alter or un-verify a sealed observation.

      THE DOCUMENTED PRIVACY CRISIS · SCIENCE (2017)
      
“Do not publish.”

      

        In Science (Vol. 356, DOI: 10.1126/science.aan1362), Lindenmayer & Scheele warned that open-access sightings of rare species are routinely mined by poachers within weeks of publication. Shielded anchoring solves the paradox: citable provenance without a poaching map.

Implemented field workflow

        
    
          
  • The full field loop in your browser: seal, tamper, sync refused, restore, and three Anchored · VERIFIED records.
    • 
          
  • Shielded memo anchors: mode B is the default; refused sends do not downgrade. Verification checks transaction id, height, confirmations and the memo. The service database prevents duplicate commitments.
    • 
          
  • Human-in-the-loop provenance: corrections are sealed beside the model’s suggestion so neither can overwrite the other.
    • 
          
  • Server-hosted Gemma 3 4B inference: verified end-to-end over an OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Live, with stated caveats

        
    
          
  • Embedded Hornet block store on Fly: the application copy and block store are separate data structures (so tampering still triggers PAYLOAD_MISMATCH), but they run in the same process. /health honestly reports independent_source: false.
    • 
          
  • Regtest chain for Zcash demos: the shielded anchor service is node-agnostic (one RPC URL), verified on a private v6.20.0 regtest chain we mine; public testnet/mainnet deployment is next.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Planned · Not built yet

        
    
          
  • On-phone local model execution: inference currently uses a reachable server endpoint; on-device WebGPU/GGUF execution is unmeasured.
    • 
          
  • Shielded field rewards: TAZ payout mechanism is designed (docs/SHIELDED_REWARDS_DESIGN.md), not claimed in the demo.
    • 
          
  • Darwin Core export is planned. Single-record JSON disclosure is implemented; account viewing keys are not exported.
    • 
        

      

    

    

      Integrity is not accuracy. Verification detects changes against a sealed commitment; it does not establish capture time or observer identity.
